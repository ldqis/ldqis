On October 14, [Dmitrii Korobeinikov](/people/dmitrii-korobeinikov/), [AJ Barea](/people/aj-barea/),
and [Dr. Leon Reznik](/people/leon-reznik/) presented "Federated Learning for the Real World:
Privacy, Security, and Cost, Without the Trade-offs" at the
[Rochester Security Summit 2026](https://www.rochestersecurity.org/), the 20th edition of the
region's security conference, run by the Rochester chapter of ISSA.

Federated learning keeps customer data on the devices that hold it, which helps with privacy and
compliance. Keeping data local does not make a system safe: an attacker who takes over a few
devices can corrupt the shared model. The talk gave practitioners a decision framework for
federated learning applications that detect and respond to attacks, weigh what each defense costs
against model performance, and adapt as conditions change, without a dedicated research team.

For the lab's research in this area, see [InteFL](/projects/intefl/), whose experiment record is
on its project page, and our [IEEE Intelligent Systems paper](/publications/intefl-mis-2026/) on
federated learning applications that monitor and adapt to changing conditions.
