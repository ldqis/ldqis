The lab's website now lives at [dataqualitylabs.com](https://dataqualitylabs.com/). Its source
code moved into the lab's own GitHub organization, [ldqis](https://github.com/ldqis), so the site
belongs to the lab rather than to one member's account. Links to the previous address,
ldqis.github.io/ldqis, forward here automatically.

Lab members can keep their own entries current in a few minutes:

- **Browser editor.** Sign in at [dataqualitylabs.com/admin](https://dataqualitylabs.com/admin/)
  with GitHub to edit your profile, projects, publications, and news posts through a form. It
  needs write access to the repository; ask [AJ Barea](/people/aj-barea/) to add you.
- **No account needed.** The [submission forms](https://github.com/ldqis/ldqis/issues/new/choose)
  for profiles, projects, and publications turn what you enter into a change for review.

The full guide is in [how to add your work](https://github.com/ldqis/ldqis#adding-your-work).
