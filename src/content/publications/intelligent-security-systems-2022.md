Dr. Leon Reznik synthesizes artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science and applies them to computer security: how to harden firewalls, intrusion detection systems, attack-recognition tools, and malware protection, and how those same techniques work both for and against defenders. Written for students and practitioners, the book bridges cybersecurity education and modern data science, with companion slides, sample questions, and exercises on the author's site.
